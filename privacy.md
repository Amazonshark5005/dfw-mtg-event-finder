# Privacy Policy — DFW MTG Event Finder

**Effective date:** October 17, 2026

## 1. Overview

DFW MTG Event Finder ("the Bot") is a community project intended to help users discover Magic: The Gathering events in the Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas area. This policy explains the information the Bot may process when used in a Discord server.

The final data practices depend on the version deployed and the hosting/configuration choices made by the operator. This policy should be checked against the deployed version and updated if its data practices change.

## 2. Information the Bot may process

Depending on enabled features and configuration, the Bot may process:

- **Discord identifiers:** server (guild) IDs and channel IDs needed to register or deliver event updates.
- **Command interaction data:** the command used and options submitted, such as a distance range, event type, or game format, so the Bot can respond.
- **Event preferences and configuration:** filters or settings selected by a user or server, if the deployed version stores them between uses.
- **Technical information:** error details or operational logs generated by the Bot or its hosting platform, which may include timestamps and limited request or diagnostic metadata.
- **Public event information:** event names, store or venue names, addresses, dates, formats, prices, and URLs obtained from configured event sources.

The Bot is not intended to request Discord passwords, payment-card details, or government identification.

## 3. How information is used

Information processed by the Bot may be used to:
- Respond to commands and apply requested event filters.
- Send scheduled event digests to a configured Discord channel.
- Remember server configuration or preferences, if that feature is enabled.
- Diagnose errors, protect the Bot from abuse, and maintain reliable operation.
- Retrieve, organize, and display event information from configured sources.

## 4. Storage and retention

Information may be processed in memory while the Bot handles a command. Server settings, preferences, or logs may also be stored by the deployed application or its hosting provider, depending on its configuration.

Retention periods depend on the actual deployment. The operator should configure logs and stored settings to retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for operation, troubleshooting, or legal obligations, and delete them when no longer needed.

## 5. Sharing and third-party services

The Bot does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by services necessary to operate it, such as Discord, the application's hosting provider, and configured event-data providers. Those services handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Event listings may link to third-party sites. If you follow those links, the destination site's privacy practices apply.

## 6. Discord

The Bot operates through Discord. Discord may independently collect and process information under its own policies. This policy does not replace Discord's Privacy Policy or Terms of Service.

## 7. Security

The operator should use reasonable safeguards for application credentials, configuration, and stored data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Users should not send secrets or sensitive personal information to the Bot.

## 8. Children's privacy

The Bot is a general community utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the operator, contact the project maintainer so the matter can be reviewed.
